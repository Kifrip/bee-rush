# Bee Rush
A one-minute top-down nectar dash: route through blooming flowers, chain collections, and weave past hornets.

User-authorized scope: complete single-player browser game; 60 seconds, 3 health, rising difficulty each 15 seconds, combo scoring, saved best, desktop and mobile. No deployment, monetization, backend, external runtime services, or paid generation.

Move with WASD/arrows or a floating touch joystick. Flowers award 10 × multiplier, grow back after 3.2 seconds, and can be collected by contact. Collect within 4 seconds to continue a streak. Every three consecutive flowers raises the multiplier, capped at 5. Damage resets it; 1.7 seconds of immunity prevents repeated hits. Survive 60 seconds to finish the harvest; zero health ends early. Only best score persists. One garden, one player, one enemy family. Pause freezes the round; switching tabs pauses automatically.

Art: original pixel matrices, bright meadow greens, honey yellow, lilac and coral blooms, cream/purple accessible DOM UI. Two-frame flying insects, swaying flowers, drifting pollen, collected nectar bursts, subtle hit shake, fade-in and animated results. Audio: original multi-phrase chiptune with bass and arpeggios, plus collection, damage, difficulty and end cues. Procedural Web Audio, activated by input.

Engine Phaser 3, JavaScript, Vite. Test pure rules with node:test and browser gameplay through Game Creator CDP helpers. GitHub Pages configuration is manual-dispatch only. No open design blockers. User explicitly requested autonomous full delivery; routine idea-phase reconfirmations and single-feature session limits are superseded by that instruction.
